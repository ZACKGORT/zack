Adding Images

Tips

To replace any placeholder image with a real photo, follow these steps.

How to add your own photos

Step 1. Go to your GitHub repository in the browser.

Step 2. Click "Add file" → "Upload files". Drag your photo in and click "Commit changes".

Step 3. In your HTML file, find the image line and change the src="..." to just the filename:

<!-- Before --> <img src="https://placehold.co/900x675/..."> <!-- After --> <img src="my-photo.jpg">
Image sizes to aim forProject main image: 900×675px · Small overlay image: 400×400px · Case study hero: 1600×700px · About portrait: 533×800px

Making Words Italic

Tips
How the italic + accent color works

Throughout the site, certain words appear in terracotta italic. You control which word by wrapping it in <em> and </em> tags.

<!-- Regular word --> I find the true story in every brand. <!-- Italic + terracotta --> I find the <em>true story</em> in every brand.
Preview

I find the true story in every brand.
