Elevation System — no drop shadows in this system
Level 0 — Flat
no shadow · no border
Default for all cards, buttons, and sections. The dominant treatment — ~95% of surfaces.
Level 1 — Hairline
1px solid {colors.hairline} #cacacb
Filter row separators, footer column borders, PDP disclosure-row separators.
Level 2 — Inset Bottom
box-shadow: inset 0 -1px 0 {colors.hairline-soft}
Sticky utility bar bottom edge, tab strip underline. The only "shadow" in the system.
Depth Principles
Depth comes entirely from photography, not CSS effects. Editorial campaign tiles create depth via cinematic perspective; product cards are shot flat on {colors.soft-cloud} so the product is the only thing with form.
Cards do not lift on hover or focus. Never introduce drop shadows or card elevation. The only interactive depth cue is the 1px inset hairline on sticky bars.
The contrast between full-bleed photography and the {colors.soft-cloud} product backdrop is the system's primary depth signal — no additional effects required.
