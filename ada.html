TikTok For Business

One platform. Every kind of advertiser.

Client
TikTok For Business
Agency
Code & Theory
Role

Senior User Experience Designer on the TikTok for Business web ecosystem.

Partnered with strategy, content, engineering and TikTok stakeholders to unify the experience globally.

Problem

TikTok for Business had grown into a fragmented network of individual B2B sites. Content was hard to manage, harder to find, and every audience was left to piece together its own path.

Headless Architecture

We designed a robust headless content architecture to streamline how information is managed and served globally, so one source of truth could feed every surface.

Ecosystem Consolidation

The objective: take the scattered B2B sites and merge them into one unified web experience.

Intent-Led Journeys

We moved the platform to an editorial, intent-driven content model, mapping and reorganizing navigation for SMBs, enterprise brands, agencies and independent creators.

Business Impact

An 80% lift in click-through for SMBs looking to start advertising.

The restructuring paid off across the whole portal: a 33% rise in views for inspiration and creative case-study sections, and an 18.5% increase in overall blog views. Better paths, better engagement.

See the live case study at zack.cx/tiktok

BNY Mellon — Pershing X

Design systems, dashboards, data, & due diligence.

Client
BNY Mellon — Pershing X
Agency
Publicis Sapient
Role

Senior User Experience Designer, Product Design Lead (IA/flows) for Wove's advisor experience.

Partnered with PM, Tech Lead, Data, Compliance, and designers to ship unified, cross-workflow journeys.

Problem

Advisors worked across many disconnected tools; context was lost and data had to be re-entered. We sought to flatten the experience through a uniform and interconnected dashboard

Vision

Build Wove, an industry-first platform that connects a client's full financial picture with the tools advisors actually use, so work moves across modules without reentry.

My Contributions

Information architecture, task and flow mapping, cross-module navigation, and component patterns that keep data alive across steps (no reentry).

Outcome

Wove launched with BNY Mellon power, scale, and security, helping advisors work faster and serve more clients.

Deep Dive

No Artificial Intelligence Here

This is important. This was only 2023 into 2024, before the buzzword craze and subsequent hype. But, while the world gets distracted by the wow-factor of AI developments, AI had ZERO part in any of this project. It was HUMAN blood, sweat, and copious amounts of caffeine that did the due diligence of analyzing every individual legacy application, mapping that **** out, socializing it with the team, and ultimately shipping a viable modernized financial product ecosystem to one of the largest banking entities in the world.

General Nutrition Centers (GNC)

This is the Whey

Client
General Nutrition Centers (GNC)
Agency
Elva Design Group
Role

Senior User Experience Designer

Problem

As part of a total overhaul and overcoming a recent bankruptcy, GNC asked us to shake things up; providing them with a modern and modular e-commerce solution through a ground-up build on the Shopify platform.

Scope

We dove deep into GNC's e-commerce ecosystem. From market research, to [client-supplied] user data, to wireframe feature prototyping; we built a modular component-based design system optimized for continued optimization.

Heavy lift. Huge Gains.

Lead UX IA/UI Prototypes/Design Systems. Paired with PM, Dev Lead, Design Team and Client-Side Stakeholders. 12-week engagement meant tight cadence, fast-on-our-feet design + strategy, and weekly exec reviews.

What We Shipped

We introduced a modern mobile-first navigation schema and retail taxonomy strategy that separated GNC not just from its competition, but all in the e-retail space.

Outcome

The result helped GNC reclaim ~$500M in annual revenue.
