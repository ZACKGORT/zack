I think. I write. I make by design.

Terms · Manifesto · Design philosophy

The sentence is the core primitive for narrative, reasoning, and presence. Interfaces should track attention the way a good editor tracks argument — not as analytics chrome, but as craft.

I build immersive, intentional systems over passive feeds: spatial memory, particle timelines, progressive narrative engines, and governance surfaces that make intent legible.

Eighteen years across agencies and product orgs — fintech, ecommerce, design systems, enterprise AI. Always toward presence: the moment someone is actually there with the work.
