About

Born April 1st.
Still not a joke.

I started at Havas Kyiv as an intern and left as a copywriter. Then Dentsu Ukraine, then Bettersvit — where things got interesting. Global FMCG, beauty, luxury, pharma. Categories that sound clinical until you remember they're about people's lives.

One campaign I wrote for the Ministry of Environmental Protection went viral nationwide on zero budget — driven entirely by participation. The holding I worked at was recognized as the most efficient agency on the market that year.

I'm based in New York. I find ideas in comment sections, one-star reviews, overheard subway conversations, the back of a shampoo bottle. The insight worth making a campaign around is usually already out there, waiting for someone to say it right.

Ministry of Environmental Protection of Ukraine

The Plastic Bag Divorce

The brief was simple and nearly impossible: persuade Ukrainians to give up single-use plastic. Zero budget. A few months to do it. Nobody stops scrolling for environmental news — but a public breakup? That travels fast. We created a social media persona for Plastic Bag and launched a nationwide divorce flash mob. Opinion leaders joined first. Then everyone else followed.
